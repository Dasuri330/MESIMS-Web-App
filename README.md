# MESIMS-Web-App
MESIMS Web-App, or the Meridian Elementary School Information Management System, is an Angular-based platform that replaces the manual, paper-driven processes of a public elementary school with a single centralized system.

The application covers the full academic workflow: parents submit enrollment forms online, registrars review and approve supporting documents, teachers encode grades by quarter, and the system computes weighted averages and generates report cards automatically. Parents and students access grades and announcements through their own portals.

Access is scoped by role. Administrators, principals, registrars, teachers, parents, and students each see only what their position permits, and every record change is written to an audit log.

DepEd compliance

Account creation and enrollment follow Department of Education requirements, including Learner Reference Number handling, PSA birth certificate and Form 138 submission, PRC license verification for teaching personnel, the six-hour daily teaching load cap under RA 4670, and the grading and descriptor system defined in DepEd Order No. 8, s. 2015.

Tech stack

Angular · TypeScript · Bootstrap 5 · ng-bootstrap
